# Collections: your project workspace

A **Collection** is the only user-facing project in the MSQ DX suite. Capabilities such as CHECKION, AUDION, BRANDION, CREATION, and METRON work *inside* that Collection — they are not separate project systems.

## What you do here

1. Create or open a Collection from Projects.
2. Invite teammates who should see that Collection (Access Model B).
3. Open a capability when you need that specialty (scan, persona, brand, compose, KPI).

## What not to say

Avoid “CHECKION project” or “AUDION project” as if they were top-level workspaces. Local product data is always bound to the Collection.
