# Platform Assistant: get help without leaving the app

The **Platform Assistant** is the suite chat for admin and product help. Open it from the floating action button — a dock-end flyout keeps your current screen visible.

## Everyday use

1. Click the Assistant FAB.
2. Ask in natural language; Collection context is used when the host publishes it.
3. Expand to `/assistant` only when you need history-heavy or long workflows — it is the **same** conversation, not a second chat.

## Not the same as

Audion **persona chat** (`/chat`) is editorial conversation with personas. It stays separate from the Platform Assistant.
