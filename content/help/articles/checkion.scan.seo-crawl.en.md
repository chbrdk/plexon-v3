# Run an SEO coverage crawl

SEO in CHECKION launches a **host-wide crawl** and opens the domain magazine with an **SEO coverage** focus. It is not a ranking promise — it shows consistency of titles, meta, headings, and related signals across the corpus.

## Steps

1. Open CHECKION from your Collection → Scan.
2. Choose **SEO**.
3. Enter the host root (or a deliberate entry point).
4. Confirm the Collection.
5. **Launch SEO crawl** and wait for **Completed** in **Jobs**.
6. Open the domain Overview → **SEO coverage**, then drill into page samples for concrete URLs.

## How to read coverage

- Prefer **recurring gaps with high page reach** (e.g. missing titles) over one perfect page.
- Other domain chapters may appear because the crawl shares a corpus — treat them as context.
- Unmeasured fields are not “pass”.
- An incomplete crawl is not a finished SEO grade.

## Product note

There is no separate SEO-only pipeline today: the launch is an SEO-focused domain crawl over the shared corpus.
